Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

House Party (published on Google Play as House Party Offline) is a collection of offline, pass-and-play party games. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

In short: House Party does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no internet access, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app does not request internet access and contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software. There is no server for the app to send data to, even if it wanted to.

Information you enter into the app

When you set up a game, you may type in player names or adjust game settings (word packs, player counts, house rules). This information is saved only in local storage on your own device, purely so the app can function — for example, remembering your last roster between launches. It is:

  • never transmitted off your device,
  • never seen by us,
  • never shared with any third party,
  • deleted automatically when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

Third-party services

House Party does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing services.

Children's privacy

Because House Party collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children either. The app contains no chat, no account creation, and no way to contact other people through it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever adds a feature that changes what's described here, we'll update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above before that feature ships.
